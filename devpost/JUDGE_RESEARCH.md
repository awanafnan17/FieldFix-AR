2. **[1. Official competition rule]** **Two-Stage Evaluation Process:**
   - **Stage One (Pass/Fail Baseline Viability):** Determines via pass/fail whether the project reasonably applies the required developer tools (`Devpost Learn skill pack`, `CLAD plugin`, `Lens Studio`, and `Lens Studio MCP`) and satisfies all Project Requirements and Submission Requirements ([Official Rules §6, Stage One](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).

3. **[1. Official competition rule]** **Deterministic Tie-Breaking Hierarchy (`Design` First):** Under Official Rules §6 (*Tie Breaking*), if two or more Submissions are tied on overall score, *"the tied Submission with the highest score in the first applicable criterion listed above will be considered the higher scoring Submission"*, repeating sequentially down the ordered list: **1st Tie-Breaker: `Design` $\rightarrow$ 2nd Tie-Breaker: `Technical Implementation` $\rightarrow$ 3rd Tie-Breaker: `Innovation/Idea` $\rightarrow$ 4th Tie-Breaker: `Presentation`** ([Official Rules §6](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).
4. **[1. Official competition rule]** **Video-First Judging Reality:** Official Rules §4 (*Testing*) and the Overview page explicitly state: *"Reviewers won't clone or run your project, so the video has to show it working"* and *"Judges are not required to test the Project and may choose to judge based solely on the text description, images, and video provided in the Submission"* ([Learn AR with SPECS Overview](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/), [Official Rules §4](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).
5. **[4. Analyst hypothesis]** Because `Design` is the #1 tie-breaker in Official Rules §6 while `Presentation` (the demo video and written pitch) is the primary medium through which judges experience the project without cloning the repo, a first-place submission must make its spatial design ergonomics, CLAD/MCP + LEAF technical verification, and spatial innovation visually unmistakable inside the demo video itself.

## 3. Criterion-by-Criterion Breakdown of Explicit Judging Expectations

### Criterion 1: Design (25% Weight | #1 Tie-Breaker)
- **[1. Official competition rule]** **Official Prompt:** *"Does the project deliver a complete, coherent product experience, not just a technical proof of concept?"* ([Official Rules §6](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).
- **[2. Direct public professional statement]** **The Four AR Design Habits (from Concept Video `S6w4aRVGQxs` & `ar-design-rules.md`):** The Overview page states: *"Watch the concept video (at the top of this page). It's short, and it covers the design ideas the judges look for"* ([Overview](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/), [`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6w4aRVGQxs`](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S6w4aRVGQxs)). In [`ar-design-rules.md`](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs/blob/main/skills/plan-lens/references/ar-design-rules.md), Joe Holmes codifies the exact rules from that video:
  1. **Habit 1 — Not a website floating in front of your face; put information on the thing it's about:** Avoid head-locked 2D dashboards; anchor status and guidance on or right next to the 3D task object.
  2. **Habit 2 — Not everything on display all the time; show it when needed, then get out of the way:** Looking at a glasses display is an active physical choice; use progressive disclosure so every glance earns its place.
  3. **Habit 3 — Not a menu full of small buttons; a few big targets ("Pinch is the new tap"):** Hands are noisy input; provide large, forgiving targets ($\ge 2^\circ$ angular size, i.e., $\ge 4.0\text{ cm}$ min / $6.0\text{ cm}$ best at $z = 110\text{ cm}$ per [Snap Positioning & Sizing Docs](https://developers.snap.com/spectacles/best-practices/design-for-spectacles/positioning-sizing-content)).
  4. **Habit 4 — Not a panel behind your shoulder; keep it where they're already looking:** SPECS has a narrow binocular FOV ($\sim 37^\circ \text{ tall} \times 32^\circ \text{ wide}$). At $z = 110\text{ cm}$ (the $1\text{ m}$ focus plane default), the usable binocular window is **$53\text{ cm high} \times 77\text{ cm wide}$** ([`ar-design-rules.md:21-24`](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs/blob/main/skills/plan-lens/references/ar-design-rules.md), [Snap Positioning & Sizing Docs](https://developers.snap.com/spectacles/best-practices/design-for-spectacles/positioning-sizing-content)).
- **[2. Direct public professional statement]** **Additive Waveguide Display Readability:** Because SPECS waveguides are additive, pure black (`#000000`) renders completely transparent; UI must use bright, high-contrast foreground text over semi-opaque backing panels/pills ([`ar-design-rules.md:27`](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs/blob/main/skills/plan-lens/references/ar-design-rules.md), [Snap Design Best Practices](https://developers.snap.com/spectacles/best-practices/design-for-spectacles/design-best-practices)).
- **[3. Independent technical evidence]** **How `FieldFix AR` V5 Aligns:** `FieldFix AR` anchors both zones at the optimal $z = -110\text{ cm}$ convergence plane within the $53\text{ cm} \times 77\text{ cm}$ binocular window (`EquipmentUnitRoot` at $x = -12.5\text{ cm}$, `DiagnosticUIRoot` `28 × 43.2 cm` `Frame` at $x = +11.5\text{ cm}$), uses $5.2 \times 5.2 \times 7.0\text{ cm}$ 3D SIK hotspot colliders ($\ge 4.4\text{ cm}$ world size at $108\text{ cm}$), renders bright cyan/white/amber/emerald text over dark backing pills (`alpha = 0.85–0.94`), tethers `[PINCH: ...]` action prompts directly onto the 3D machine, and uses progressive disclosure (`uiDisclosureMode = "onboarding"`) so repair steps and upstream answers are not spoiled before inspection ([`devpost/design-brief.md`](./design-brief.md), [`devpost/VERIFICATION_REPORT.md`](./VERIFICATION_REPORT.md)).

### Criterion 3: Innovation / Idea (25% Weight | #3 Tie-Breaker)
- **[1. Official competition rule]** **Official Prompt:** *"How creative and novel is the concept and does the project differ from existing concepts?"* ([Official Rules §6](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).
- **[2. Direct public professional statement]** **How Originality & Spatial Necessity Are Evaluated:**

Great AR combines them."*
  2. Independent technical evidence]** **How `FieldFix AR` V5 Aligns:** Rather than presenting a flat 2D checklist (which could live on a phone or tablet), `FieldFix AR` requires spatial 3D reasoning: trainees observe 2-zone differential coolant starvation across a 3D manifold, trace the fault upstream in reverse-causal order (`TR-309` overheat symptom $\rightarrow$ `PR-204` `142 PSI` vapor-lock bottleneck $\rightarrow$ `CV-101` `18%` intake cavitation root cause), trigger **localized 3D Auto-X-Ray Cutaways** that elevate outer housings in 3D space to expose internal valve seals, relief springs, and stator coils, and experience **consequence-driven LOTO safety interlock enforcement** (`85% B+` vs. `100% A+` competency scorecard) ([`devpost/SIGNATURE_EXPERIENCE_PROPOSAL.md`](./SIGNATURE_EXPERIENCE_PROPOSAL.md)).

### 5.2 Preview Testing vs. Physical SPECS Hardware & Simulation Scope Disclosure
1. **[1. Official competition rule]** **No Physical Glasses Required:** The Overview page, Resources FAQ, and Official Rules §4 explicitly confirm that physical SPECS hardware is **not** required: *"Do I need SPECS glasses? No. You build and test everything in Lens Studio's preview, and your demo video is a recording of the preview"* ([Resources FAQ](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/resources), [Official Rules §4](https://learn-specs.devpost.com/rules)).
2. **[2. Direct public professional statement]** **Required Epistemic Honesty ("Logic Proven" vs. "Feel Unproven"):** In [`templates/design-brief-template.md:72`](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs/blob/main/skills/plan-lens/templates/design-brief-template.md) and [`references/preview-limits.md:34`](https://github.com/challengepost/learn-specs/blob/main/skills/plan-lens/references/preview-limits.md), Joe Holmes instructs builders to distinguish between what the preview proves (`"logic proven"`) and what requires physical glasses or real-world sensors (`"feel unproven"`).
3. **[3. Independent technical evidence]** **How `FieldFix AR` V5 Complies:** Both [`devpost/SUBMISSION.md`](./SUBMISSION.md) and [`devpost/design-brief.md`](./design-brief.md) include an explicit **Simulation Scope Note** stating that `FieldFix AR` is an interactive maintenance training simulation tested in the Lens Studio `5.24.1` (`SPECS 27`) preview, where all telemetry (`°C`, `PSI`, `Flow %`) and `LOTO` interlocks are simulated in software for procedural training (**logic proven** via `5/5` LEAF scenarios and SIK preview interaction) rather than interfacing with physical plant sensors or physical optical waveguide hardware (**feel unproven** on physical glasses).
